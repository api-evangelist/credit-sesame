# Credit Sesame

> Credit Sesame is a consumer financial health platform offering free daily credit-score monitoring, credit reports, and personalized recommendations for credit cards, loans, and insurance, plus a Sesame+ premium tier with three-bureau reports and identity-theft protection. Credit Sesame is a company profile in the API Evangelist network; it operates consumer web and mobile products and does not publish a public developer API at this time.

## Company
- [Credit Sesame](https://www.creditsesame.com): Consumer credit and financial health platform.
- [Sesame Grade](https://www.creditsesame.com/sesame-grade/): Credit health grading.
- [Sesame+](https://www.creditsesame.com/sesame-plus-3-bureau-credit-reports/): Premium three-bureau reports and identity-theft protection.
